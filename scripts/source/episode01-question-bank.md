### RD01 — Easy — Travel

**Passage:**\
Travelling by train can be a practical way to move between European cities. Passengers can often travel directly from one city centre to another without dealing with airport security or long transfers. However, journey times and ticket prices vary depending on the route and time of travel.

**Question:** What is one advantage of travelling by train mentioned in the passage?

A. It is always cheaper than flying\
B. It can connect city centres directly\
C. It never requires a ticket\
D. Journey times are always shorter

**Correct:** B

---

### RD02 — Easy — Urban Environment

**Passage:**\
Many cities are redesigning streets to make walking and cycling easier. Measures may include wider pavements, protected cycle lanes and safer crossings. Supporters argue that these changes can improve everyday mobility while reducing dependence on private cars for short journeys.

**Question:** What is the main purpose of the measures described?

A. To increase private car use\
B. To make walking and cycling easier\
C. To eliminate public transport\
D. To make all journeys longer

**Correct:** B

---

### RD03 — Medium — Tourism

**Passage:**\
Tourism can bring significant economic benefits to a destination. Hotels, restaurants, transport operators and local shops may all benefit from visitor spending. However, destinations can also experience overcrowding, increased waste and pressure on natural or historic sites during busy periods.

**Question:** What is one potential negative effect of tourism?

A. Lower visitor spending\
B. Pressure on natural and historic sites\
C. Fewer local businesses\
D. Less demand for accommodation

### RD04 — Medium — Mountain Environment

B. Temperatures always increase with altitude\
