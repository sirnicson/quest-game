# QUEST V3.01 — Episode 01 Gameplay Core

Development baseline: QUEST v2.0.0 locked.

First cumulative gameplay slice:
- Episode 01 starts at London / START.
- 50:00 session timer starts when PLAY EPISODE 01 is pressed.
- Stop 01 is a Quiz / Quick Win.
- Player selects an answer and checks it.
- Correct answer: traveller advances +1 and Stop 02 becomes active.
- Incorrect answer: traveller holds position and the challenge enters recovery/rematch state.
- Correct/incorrect gameplay audio cues fire through the existing audio system.
- Existing V2 opening, map, branding and cinematic systems remain intact.

V2 remains the fallback/source of truth until V3 is explicitly approved and locked.


# QUEST V3.02 — London Gameplay Entry

Cumulative gameplay refinement from V3.01:
- PLAY EPISODE 01 now enters the gameplay map with a dedicated London camera phase.
- The map camera zooms into London / START instead of opening the old split map-and-panel layout.
- Both selected traveller avatars are offset beside START at the opening position.
- START is the explicit entry point into Stop 01.
- Challenge and result cards are embedded as overlays on the map, keeping the journey visible underneath.
- The existing adventure soundtrack transition and gameplay audio remain intact.
- The locked V2 baseline remains untouched and available as rollback source.
